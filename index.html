what's real here?

Exact: the Kerr metric (Kerr–Schild form) — an exact solution of Einstein's vacuum field equations. Photon and particle paths are its geodesics, integrated numerically (RK4). Lensing, the shadow, the photon ring, frame dragging, periapsis precession, Doppler beaming (g⁴) and gravitational redshift all emerge from the equations.

Modeled: the disk is an idealized thin disk (Novikov–Thorne-style temperature profile, Keplerian circular orbits, procedural turbulence for texture). Real disks need magnetohydrodynamics on supercomputers.

Display: intensities are tone-mapped — a real disk's dynamic range would blind you.

Cinematic look: mutes Doppler beaming and color shifts and uses a warm gold palette — the same artistic liberty the Interstellar team took for Gargantua. Untick it for the physically colored disk: blazing blue-white on the approaching side, dim red on the receding side.
